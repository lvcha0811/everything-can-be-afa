# 阿发角色圣经

Use this hierarchy across photorealistic, comic, pixel-art, poster, mascot, and 3D treatments.

## Fixed identity

- Character: 阿发 / Afa, one specific young red Shiba Inu—not a generic dog.
- Coat: warm tan-orange with cream/white muzzle, cheeks, chest, legs, and light forehead-to-eyebrow transitions.
- Face: youthful and moderately round, natural width, upright proportionate ears, black nose, almond-shaped dark eyes, calm and slightly composed expression. The occasional one-eye squint is a reference expression, not a permanent anatomical trait.
- Body: believable Shiba scale and anatomy; compact but not squat, with canine paws and a curled tail whenever visible.
- Name tag: yellow fabric plate/tag with black Chinese text `阿发`; retain the small cartoon Shiba-head motif when resolution allows.
- Harness: a selectable wardrobe element, not a single fixed color. Use exactly one of the approved variants below.

## Approved harness variants

| Key | User language | Appearance | Best reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mint` | 绿色、薄荷绿、蓝绿色 | pale aqua/mint body with turquoise straps and light buckles | `afa-front-mint-harness-yellow-tag.png` |
| `neon-yellow` | 黄色、荧光黄、黄绿色 | high-visibility fluorescent yellow/lime, sometimes with pink straps | `afa-three-quarter-neon-yellow-harness-tag.png` |
| `light-blue` | 浅蓝、蓝色胸背 | soft powder-blue mesh harness | `afa-sleeping-light-blue-harness.jpg` |

Do not combine mint body panels, neon-yellow panels, and light-blue mesh in one harness unless the user explicitly requests a hybrid design.

## Identity priority

1. Face and coat pattern.
2. Yellow tag reading `阿发` exactly.
3. One correctly selected harness variant.
4. Natural Shiba body, paws, and curled tail.
5. Minor accessories such as a small pendant.

## Style adaptation

- Photorealistic: preserve fur, anatomy, optics, and environmental lighting.
- Comic/flat illustration: simplify texture but retain facial proportions, cream markings, harness color, and tag.
- Pixel/perler art: retain identity as color blocks; render `阿发` only when resolution supports legibility.
- 3D mascot/poster: allow expressive staging while preserving canine paws and Afa's facial proportions.
- Meme/minimal graphic: simplify the scene before simplifying identity anchors.

## Forbidden drift

- Generic Shiba face; elderly, overweight, cat-like, or permanently wide-eyed appearance.
- Human hands, feet, or full human proportions unless explicitly requested.
- Mixed harness palettes, wrong requested color, or a chest placard replacing the real tag.
- Misspelled, substituted, mirrored, decorative, or falsely claimed `阿发` text.
- Unrequested breed, coat, age, costume, or personality changes.
